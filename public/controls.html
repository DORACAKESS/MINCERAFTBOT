Quick Bot Behaviours

Controls

One-tap behaviours — Follow keeps the bot at your side, Guard defends you, Mining digs tunnels, plus Sleep and Look.

Follow Player

The bot follows a player automatically — walking in survival, or teleporting on an operator server.

When they move farther than the radius below, the bot follows. When they come back inside it, the bot stops.

Survival walks and hops over obstacles. Operator instantly /tp's when they leave the radius (needs OP on the server).

The bot moves whenever the target is farther than this many blocks away (1–128).

Follow settings persist across bot restarts.

Guard Player

Follow a player AND fight off threats near them. Same movement as Follow, plus attack filters.

The bot follows them like Follow, and attacks anything the filters allow.

Attack filters

Hostile mobs are attacked on sight. Passive mobs and players are only attacked in retaliation — if they hit the bot or the guarded player. Unchecked = that group is completely ignored (even retaliation).

Guard and Follow cannot run at the same time — saving one turns the other off.

Mining

The bot digs a tunnel straight ahead — a level 1×2 tunnel, or a 1:1 staircase descending underground.

Straight mines the wall at body height and walks forward. Stair digs the wall and the step below it, descending one block every step — great for reaching ores underground.

Mining stops Follow/Guard while it runs (they share the movement keys).

Quick actions

Follow and Guard are applied the moment you save.

Turn the behaviour off without touching your saved settings.

Sleep

Make the bot go to bed — it scans for the nearest bed, walks over and sleeps.

Sleep only works at night (or during a thunderstorm) and when no monsters are nearby — the bot will tell you why if it can't.

Look at Players

Turn the bot's head toward the nearest player — a fun idle behaviour, no movement involved.

When on, the bot looks at the nearest player within 32 blocks every second. It never walks — for idle moments: while Follow or Guard is active they own the bot's head instead, and it pauses during Sleep. Saved instantly.

Controls run live on the bot · settings persist across restarts
